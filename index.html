What we do

From raw field data to deployed AI models

The center assists researchers in adopting and integrating AI and machine learning tools into their work, from data collection and management to model development and deployment. By providing access to high-performance computing, advanced sensing technologies, and methodological guidance, the AI Center enables data-driven research and accelerates innovation in agriculture.

High-performance computing

A dedicated cluster of CPU and GPU servers with large-scale fast storage for training and running complex AI models.

Advanced sensing

Hyperspectral, thermal, LiDAR and field phenotyping systems that capture high-resolution data in the lab and in the field.

Methodological guidance

Hands-on support for researchers adopting machine learning, from study design and data management to model deployment.
