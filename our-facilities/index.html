Facilities & Infrastructure

We bridge the gap between advanced data science and practical agriculture with two core pillars: advanced remote sensing and field phenotyping instruments for high-fidelity data collection, and a High-Performance Computing (HPC) center designed to process massive datasets and train complex AI models.

Pillar 1

Remote sensing & environmental sensing systems

To fuel our machine learning and neural network models, the center is equipped with a comprehensive suite of sensing technologies. These instruments capture high-resolution spectral, spatial and environmental data in the lab and in the field:

  • Hyperspectral & thermal imaging: a mobile hyperspectral imaging system alongside advanced thermal cameras to capture precise plant signatures and water stress levels.
  • Active remote sensing & spectroscopy: a ground-based LiDAR system for detailed 3D structural mapping, a portable Raman spectroscopy system and a fluorometer for physiological and molecular analysis.
  • High-precision spatial tracking: a GPS RTK receiver for centimeter-level geospatial accuracy during data collection.
  • Microclimate & soil IoT sensors: soil sensors, stomatal conductance meters (porometers) and a mobile meteorological station to monitor environmental and plant-water dynamics in real time.
  • Field phenotyping platforms: high-resolution RGB cameras and a dedicated field phenotyping system (PhenoMobile) for high-throughput crop analytics.
Hyperspectral cameras

Two Specim hyperspectral cameras

Hyperspectral cameras record hundreds of narrow wavelength bands for every pixel, revealing chemical and physiological properties of plants and seeds that ordinary cameras cannot see.

Specim IQ

Portable visible and near-infrared (VNIR) hyperspectral camera

  • Range: 400–1000 nm, 204 spectral bands
  • Image: 512 × 512 pixels per capture
  • Portable: battery-powered, with a built-in scanner, touchscreen and processing, for the lab, greenhouse or field

Used in Anthocyanin Dynamics in Red Lettuces

Specim SWIR

Short-wave infrared (SWIR) line-scan hyperspectral camera

  • Range: 938–2542 nm, 256 spectral bands
  • Image: 320 pixels per line, scanned line by line
  • Lab platform: mounted over a moving stage with halogen lighting and in-scene reference panels

Used in MaltPrediction

Pillar 2

Generously funded by the Leona M. and Harry B. Helmsley Charitable Trust, our dedicated supercomputing hub provides the computing power needed to store, analyze and deploy next-generation AI pipelines.

Processing power

A cluster of 26 CPU servers paired with 36 high-end GPU systems, optimized for parallel processing and intensive deep learning workloads.

Scalable storage tiers

350 TB of ultra-fast flash storage for active computation and 500 TB of long-term archival storage for large historical datasets.

High-speed interconnects

Low-latency, high-bandwidth networking between processing nodes and storage tiers.

Software & AI frameworks

Full system licenses and commercial software suites that support advanced AI and data science development.
