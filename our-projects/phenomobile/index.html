PhenoMobile

A high-clearance robotic platform for ground-level crop phenotyping.

The platform

Data from inside the crop rows

The PhenoMobile is a high-clearance robotic platform custom-engineered to advance smart agriculture and meet the challenges of rapid climate change. While aerial drones offer a broad overview of agricultural land, the PhenoMobile drives directly within the crop rows. Equipped with heavy, mud-resilient tires, it delivers ultra-high-resolution data right from ground level.

Advanced sensing

Three cameras, mounted inline

To capture a complete picture of plant health, the PhenoMobile carries a specialized payload of three advanced cameras:

RGB imaging

Captures high-fidelity visual data to analyze structural crop traits, such as leaf distance, flower counts and overall plant development.

Hyperspectral sensing

Scans hundreds of light bands beyond human sight, revealing hidden biochemical indicators, nutrient distribution and early signs of physiological stress.

Thermal infrared imaging

Maps temperature across the crop canopy, diagnosing transpiration levels and water stress in real time.

Data fusion

Multi-purpose analytics

By scanning fields and collecting imagery from all three sensors at once, our center uses machine learning and artificial neural networks to fuse the data. This cross-referencing lets us calculate advanced vegetation indices, such as the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), alongside detailed thermal-to-morphological maps.

From assessing irrigation requirements and predicting soil-nutrient uptake to screening climate-resilient crop varieties, the PhenoMobile turns large fields into actionable, data-driven insights. It bridges the gap between agricultural biology and scalable artificial intelligence.
