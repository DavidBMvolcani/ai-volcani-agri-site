What CropMap produces

A classified map for a whole agricultural year

CropMap turns agricultural parcels and remote-sensing imagery into a crop and land-use map for an agricultural year (October to October). The result is a table and a GIS map layer in which every parcel carries its predicted crop or land-use class.

The AI center's role

Operating the system

The center deploys, runs, monitors and maintains the CropMap pipelines on Google Cloud.

Data sources

From space and from the air

CropMap combines three inputs: the agricultural parcel polygons, a year of Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 satellite time series, and (for the land-use engine) aerial orthophotos.

How it works

Parcels in, classified map out

  1. IngestParcel polygons are loaded into BigQuery; for the land-use engine, aerial orthophotos are converted to Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFFs.
  2. SegmentYearly harmonic composites are exported from Earth Engine, and a segmentation network splits each parcel into homogeneous sub-parcels.
  3. Satellite time seriesA full agricultural year of Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 imagery is downloaded for each sub-parcel and processed into a clean time series.
  4. ClassifyThe model runs on every sub-parcel: XGBoost for field crops, or the deep fused model for land uses and orchards.
  5. Post-processSplit segments that share a class are merged back, and the result is written as a BigQuery table and a GeoPackage map.
Segmentation

From satellite image to sub-parcels

Two classification engines

One for field crops, one for land uses and orchards

Both engines share the same first steps: parcel segmentation and satellite download and processing.

Field crops (gadash)

  • Model: XGBoost on the Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 time series and on NDVI growth cycles detected for each sub-parcel.
  • Fast, and works in season: monthly models (January to September) allow a map before the year ends.

Land uses and orchards (fused)

  • Model: a deep model that fuses two views of each sub-parcel: the Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 time series, read by a temporal attention encoder (LTAE), and orthophoto patches, read by a Swin Transformer.
  • Orchards: parcels classified as orchards are re-classified into orchard subtypes by a second model.
  • Richer, end of year: runs on a completed agricultural year.
Infrastructure

Running on Google Cloud

  • Orchestration: Apache Airflow wires each pipeline as a chain of tasks. It orchestrates but does not compute; runs are started by hand.
  • Execution: every heavy step runs as a Cloud Run job, with parallel tasks where the work allows.
  • Satellite imagery: Google Earth Engine exports the Sentinel imagery and the yearly composites.
  • Tables: BigQuery holds every intermediate and final table.
  • Storage: Google Cloud Storage holds working files and an archive of models and maps.
  • Notifications: success and failure emails for each run.
